## Brand & Style

This design system serves a dual purpose: a rigorous, peer-reviewed academic publication and an interactive, real-time multimodal evaluation workbench. It compares legacy encoder-decoder architectures (CNN-LSTM with additive soft attention) against contemporary pretrained Vision-Language models (BLIP, CLIP ViT-B/32, ViT-GPT2) with an uncompromising commitment to digital accessibility.

The aesthetic fuses **Scientific High-Contrast Precision** with **Tactile Editorial Clarity**. Rather than generic academic PDFs or sterile SaaS dashboards, the interface channels the authoritative weight of archival scientific journals reimagined through modern, WCAG AAA-compliant interface engineering. 

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Radical Legibility:** Every typographic choice, spatial relation, and contrast ratio is calibrated to exceed standard accessibility criteria, catering equally to low-vision researchers, screen-reader users, and sighted ML practitioners.
